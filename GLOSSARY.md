# CampaignGround

Campaign software for local races: one campaign's voters, people, and outreach, run by a small team.

## People

**Teammate**:
A person with access to a campaign, at one of two access levels.
_Avoid_: Member (as a general term), user, team member, staff

**Access level**:
What a teammate may see and change in a campaign: owner or member.
_Avoid_: Role (reserved for volunteer roles), permission

**Owner**:
A teammate with full access, including donors and donations.
_Avoid_: Admin, manager

**Member**:
A teammate with every access except donors and donations.
_Avoid_: Staff, collaborator

**Volunteer**:
A person on the campaign's roster who helps with outreach, whether or not they are a teammate.
_Avoid_: Helper, canvasser (as a synonym)

**Volunteer role**:
The kind of work a volunteer does, such as canvassing or phone banking.
_Avoid_: Role (unqualified), position

**Donor**:
A person or organization that gives money to the campaign; may be the same person as one voter.
_Avoid_: Contributor, funder, backer

## Outreach

**Household**:
The voters who live at one address.
_Avoid_: Residence, home

**Door**:
A household, as the unit a canvasser visits.
_Avoid_: Using "door" for an individual voter

**Contact attempt**:
One try at reaching a voter, at the door or by phone, whatever the result.
_Avoid_: Knock, call, touch

**Contact**:
A contact attempt that led to a conversation with the voter. A refusal is not a contact.
_Avoid_: Reached, canvassed, touched

**Supporter**:
A voter whose recorded support level is strong or lean support. A yard-sign request alone does not make a supporter.
_Avoid_: Backer, yes voter, base

**Walk list**:
Every door in one precinct, in street order, for canvassers to work through.
_Avoid_: Turf, route, canvass list

**County voter ID**:
The identifier the election office assigns to a registered voter.
_Avoid_: Voter number, registration ID, state ID

## Platform

**Activated campaign**:
A campaign where someone has logged at least one contact attempt on a voter.
_Avoid_: Active campaign, live campaign, onboarded
