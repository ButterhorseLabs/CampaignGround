# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 18, 2026_

CampaignGround is campaign software for local races, run by ButterhorseLabs ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the hosted service at this website collects, what we do with it, and the choices you have. It covers the hosted service only. If you run your own copy of the open-source code, you are the operator of that copy and this policy does not apply to it.

Questions or requests: **privacy@butterhorselabs.dev**.

## Two kinds of information, two different roles

**Your account.** When you sign up or accept an invite, you give us your name, email address, and a password. We decide how this information is used, and this policy describes that.

**Your campaign's records.** Campaigns put information about other people into CampaignGround: voters, volunteers, donors, donations, events, tasks, and notes from door knocks and phone calls. The campaign decides what goes in and how it is used. We store and process it only to run the service for that campaign. If you are a voter, volunteer, or donor and want to know what a campaign holds about you, contact that campaign first. If you can't reach them, email us and we will pass your request on.

## What we collect

- **Account details:** name, email address, and your password, which we store only as a one-way hash, so we cannot read it.
- **Campaign records you enter or import.** Depending on what your campaign uses, this can include:
  - names, addresses, phone numbers and email addresses
  - party affiliation, past voting participation, birth year, and support level
  - door-knock and call outcomes, notes, and yard-sign requests
  - volunteer roles and hours
  - donor employer, occupation and contribution amounts
- **Team information:** which campaigns you belong to, your access level (owner or member), and invites you have sent or received.
- **Email delivery information:** whether an email we sent could be delivered. Our email provider tells us when an address bounces.
- **Server logs:** technical records our hosting provider keeps when the service runs, such as errors. When an invite or password reset email fails, the log includes the recipient's email address.

## Cookies

We use one cookie to keep you logged in. We do not use advertising cookies, analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party scripts.

## How we use information

- To run the service: log you in, show your campaign its own records, send invites and password reset emails, and keep two volunteers from working the same household at the same time.
- To keep the service secure: limiting repeated login attempts, investigating abuse, and fixing errors.
- To operate the hosted service: we can see a list of accounts, their email addresses, which campaigns they belong to, and how far each campaign has got with setup (for example, whether it has imported voters or logged a door knock). We use this to support campaigns and improve the product. It does not include the contents of a campaign's voter, donor, or contact records.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use it for advertising. We do not share one campaign's records with any other campaign. We do not use campaign records to train AI models.

## Who can see your campaign's records

- **People you invite.** Members of your campaign can see its voters, volunteers, events, tasks and outreach records.
- **Owners only.** Only campaign owners can see donors, donations, and fundraising totals.
- **Us.** We access stored records only when needed to operate, secure, or support the service, when you ask us to, or when the law requires it.

## Where information is stored

Information is stored in the United States. If you are in Canada, your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States, and may be accessible to US authorities under US law.

## How long we keep information

We keep your account and campaign records while your account or campaign is active. There is no self-service delete button yet. To delete your account or a campaign you own, email us. We will delete it within 30 days, and it will be removed from any backups within 90 days. We may keep the minimum needed to show that we honoured a deletion request or to meet a legal obligation.

## Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:
- give you a copy of your account information, or of the records of a campaign you own
- correct your account information
- delete your account, or a campaign you own

Email privacy@butterhorselabs.dev. We will respond within 30 days.

Depending on where you live, you may have more rights under laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act or Canada's PIPEDA. In Canada, you can also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada. We honour these rights for all users, wherever they live.

## Security

We protect information with encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, and access checks on every request based on your campaign and access level. We also limit repeated requests. No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach that affects your information, we will notify affected campaign owners without undue delay.

## Age

CampaignGround is for adults. You must be 18 or older to create an account.

## Changes to this policy

If we make a material change, we will email account holders before it takes effect and update the date at the top of this page.
